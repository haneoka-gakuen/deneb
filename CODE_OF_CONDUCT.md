# Community code of conduct

We want collaboration around Deneb to be safe, technically rigorous, and welcoming.

Participants must:

- discuss ideas and code without harassment or personal attacks;
- respect identity, experience level, language, and accessibility needs;
- give actionable criticism and accept reasonable review;
- avoid publishing private information or unlicensed material;
- stop behavior that a maintainer identifies as disruptive.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security contact or maintainer contact. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with conflicts of interest avoided.
